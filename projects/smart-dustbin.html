IoT-Based Smart Dustbin

A smart dustbin prototype using ultrasonic level detection, servo-controlled lid opening, IR full detection, and Blynk app alerts when the bin reaches 90% capacity.

Problem

Bins can overflow when fill levels are not monitored, and contactless lid opening improves convenience and hygiene.

Solution

The system uses an ultrasonic sensor to estimate fill level, a servo motor for automatic lid opening, an IR sensor for bin-full confirmation, and Blynk for manual control and notifications when the bin is around 90% full.

My Contribution

  • Integrated ultrasonic sensing for fill-level estimation.
  • Implemented servo-based lid control and IR-based full detection.
  • Configured Blynk app control and notification alerts.
  • Tested bin status indication and alert behavior.

Outcome

  • Built a functional IoT prototype demonstrating sensor interfacing, actuator control, and mobile app-based monitoring.
