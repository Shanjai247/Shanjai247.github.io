EEG-Based Wearable System for Sleep Quality Assessment

A wearable EEG headband system for sleep quality assessment using a custom STM32H743-based PCB, ESP32 feature extraction, ADS1220 ADC, BioAmp EXG Pill, TinyML inference, BLE communication, and a Flutter mobile app.

  • Designed the custom 4-layer STM32H743VIT6 PCB including schematic, routing, grounding, and power distribution in EasyEDA.
